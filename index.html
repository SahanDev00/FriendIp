What happens when you continue?

This page records an approximate location from your public IP address. It will also ask your browser for permission to share your device location, which may be more accurate.

Your precise location is sent only if you choose Allow in the browser's permission prompt. If you choose Block, only the approximate IP-based location is used. Your raw IP address is not saved in the project's database.
